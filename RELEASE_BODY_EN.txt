Computational materials accompanying “Continual Recognition of Printed Polymer Contexts from Published Average Mechanical Records” by Salih Çolakoğlu and Mustafa Onat, Marmara University.

This version supplies the five-fold group-heldout task-incremental and class-incremental comparisons, simple references, Replay, EWC, LwF and Walsh-negotiated configurations. The complete 2,386-file supplement contains source data, scientific protocols, code, learned states, predictions, results, figures and plotting sources. It preserves the existing learning outcomes and historical fixed-allocation comparisons.

Five saved Simulink inference banks reproduce all 47,502 accepted source-record decisions across 2,610 logical evaluation populations. Labels, masks, ties and native coverage/error points were checked; the largest observed absolute numerical difference was 9.77e-15. Saved models were cold-reopened and their semantic state read back. No retraining or new physical experiment was performed for this check. It verifies implementation parity, without adding a physical-validation or algorithmic-accuracy claim.

All 104 published average records in 26 source-condition groups were previously inspected. Group-heldout results remain exploratory. Averages, task views and seeds are not independent specimens. Separate TaskIL and ClassIL protocols and all method variants/initializations remain visible.
