A30 — Code, data, models, results and figures

Computational materials accompanying "Continual Recognition of Printed Polymer
Contexts from Published Average Mechanical Records" by Salih Çolakoğlu and
Mustafa Onat, Marmara University.

This version supplies the five-fold group-heldout task-incremental and
class-incremental comparisons, simple references, Replay, EWC, LwF and
Walsh-negotiated configurations. The full supplement includes source data,
scientific protocols, code, learned states, predictions, results, figures and
their plotting sources. It preserves the original fixed-allocation comparisons
and the existing learning outcomes.

Five saved Simulink inference banks now reproduce all 47,502 accepted source-record
decisions across 2,610 logical evaluation populations. Labels, masks, ties and
native coverage/error points were checked; the largest observed absolute numerical
difference was9.77e-15. Saved models were cold-reopened and their semantic state
read back. No retraining or new physical experiment was performed for this parity
check. It is implementation verification, not physical validation or an additional
algorithmic accuracy claim.

All104published average records in26source-condition groups were previously
inspected. Group-heldout results remain exploratory; averages, task views and
seeds are not independent specimens. Separate TaskIL and ClassIL protocols and
all method variants/initializations are retained.
