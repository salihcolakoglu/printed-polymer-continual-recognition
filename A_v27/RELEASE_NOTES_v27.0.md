# A27 / v27.0 — Code, data, models, results and figures

Computational materials supporting “Continual Recognition of Printed Polymer Contexts from Published Average Mechanical Records” by Salih Çolakoğlu and Mustafa Onat, Marmara University.

This version brings together the preserved reference comparisons and the task-incremental and class-incremental experiments with ordinary and Walsh codes, negotiated targets, replay and joint-seen controls. It includes 104 attributed published average records in 26 processing-condition groups, all five numerical initializations, saved learning results and model states, two Simulink inference demonstrations, and eight manuscript figures with numerical plotting sources.

Relative to the verified A25 computational package, this version aligns the package and citation metadata with manuscript A27, supplies its clean-title exports for Figures 5–8, and makes the editorial plotting helper read the package's own saved summaries. Learning code, source records, model states and numerical results are preserved. No new training or simulation was performed for this packaging step. Historical component filenames remain for traceability.
